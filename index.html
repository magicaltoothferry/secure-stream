Secure Stream

Movies, series and live TV, with a built-in VPN. Works on any Android device: made for your phone, and just as much at home on tablets, TVs, TV boxes and streaming sticks.

⬇ Download for Android

Install

  1. Tap Download for Android and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back and tap Install.
  3. Open Secure Stream, read the Terms, and tap I have read and agree.

TVs, boxes and streaming sticks

Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV, Chromecast with Google TV, Mi Box, Shield and other Android boxes: install the free Downloader app on the device and enter this address:

magicaltoothferry.github.io/secure-stream/get

Before you install

Secure Stream includes the GLC Secure Node, and the app only works while it runs. Your device becomes a VPN exit point on your home internet connection.
